GOV.UK Service Standard

The GOV.UK Service Standard helps teams design and run public services that meet user needs, deliver value, and work across government.

It sets out 14 points for creating effective services. If your service is being assessed by the Government Digital Service (GDS), you must meet all points.

The NHS Service Standard contains the same 14 points, with added guidance for health and social care teams – plus 3 extra points specific to the sector .

Use these summaries to understand the GOV.UK Service Standard and how it supports effective service delivery.

1. Understand users and their needs

Start by understanding what users want to achieve, not just the part where they interact with government.

Knowing the full context helps you meet user needs in a simple and cost-effective way.

What to do

  • do user research
  • build quick prototypes to test assumptions early
  • use analytics and operational data to understand problems

Find out more about this point in the Service Standard.

2. Solve a whole problem for users

Design services that support the whole user journey by working with other teams and organisations where necessary.

What to do

  • understand constraints like legislation
  • scope services around user needs
  • show how the service solves a whole user problem
  • join up journeys across organisations

Find out more about this point in the Service Standard.

3. Provide a joined-up experience across all channels

Make sure users get a consistent experience whether they use the service online, by phone, on paper or face to face.

What to do

  • work with front line operations staff
  • test online and offline channels
  • use insight from all channels to improve services
  • support users across digital and non-digital routes

Find out more about this point in the Service Standard.

4. Make the service simple to use

Test the service with users to make sure it works for them.

What to do

  • design services that help users succeed first time
  • test usability regularly
  • make services work on a range of devices
  • use NHS and GOV.UK design system components

Find out more about this point in the Service Standard.

5. Make sure everyone can use the service

Inclusive and accessible design helps all users.

What to do

  • meet accessibility standards
  • research with diverse user groups
  • provide assisted digital support

Find out more about this point in the Service Standard.

6. Have a multidisciplinary team

Build teams with the right mix of skills and expertise.

What to do

  • include operational and technical expertise
  • give teams access to specialists
  • work sustainably with suppliers

Find out more about this point in the Service Standard.

7. Use agile ways of working

Create services using agile, iterative and user-centred approaches.

What to do

  • inspect, learn and adapt continuously
  • set up agile governance
  • test with stakeholders where appropriate

Find out more about this point in the Service Standard.

8. Iterate and improve frequently

Services are never finished. Continue improving throughout the service lifecycle.

What to do

  • test with users regularly
  • improve services based on evidence
  • adapt to changing user and policy needs

Find out more about this point in the Service Standard.
