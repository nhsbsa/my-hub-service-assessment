Prepare for a GDS assessment as a team

A Government Digital Service (GDS) assessment is a key step for digital teams delivering public services in the UK. You’ll need to show how your service meets the GDS Service Standard at each stage of delivery, including Discovery, Alpha, Beta and Live.

At the NHSBSA, our services are assessed by trained Department of Health and Social Care (DHSC) assessors through a peer review process. Their role is to check that our services meet the GDS Service Standard and are designed around user needs.

All GDS-assessed services, including those under DHSC, must be simple, accessible, and easy to use on any device. They must also support people without internet access and people with limited digital skills.

Who assesses us

The Department of Health and Social Care (DHSC) asses our services. They check digital health and social care services to make sure they meet government standards. This includes NHS services with more than 100,000 transactions each year.

DHSC use the GOV.UK Service Standard. This is a set of 14 principles for creating services that work well for users.

The Government Digital Service (GDS) checks services used by central government departments. GDS was previously called the Central Digital and Data Office (CDDO).

What you need to do

Use the My Hub guidance and checklists to help you and your team:

  • understand what is expected of a digital service
  • deliver good offline or assisted digital support
  • know what you must achieve in each phase to pass an assessment
  • understand the Service Standard and show how you meet each point
  • learn what each team member is expected to contribute when preparing and presenting at the assessment
  • prepare key documents
  • understand the structure of the assessment and how to present your service
  • get tips for Discovery, Alpha, Beta and Live
